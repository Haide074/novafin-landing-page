FAQ

Questions you are probably asking

What makes NovaFin different from other accounting firms in Dubai?

Three things. Fixed monthly pricing instead of hourly billing, an FTA-registered tax agent and chartered accountants on your file rather than juniors, and monthly advice on what your numbers mean instead of filings with no explanation.

How much does it cost?

Essential starts at AED 833.33 per month and Elite at AED 3,333.33 per month. Final pricing depends on transaction volume, entity count and sector. You get a fixed quote after the free review and it does not change unless the scope does.

Is this cheaper than hiring an accountant in-house?

Almost always. A mid-level UAE accountant costs AED 8,000 to 12,000 a month plus visa, gratuity and software, and covers one skill set. NovaFin covers bookkeeping, tax, payroll, reporting and advisory for less.

Can you take over from my current accountant?

Yes. We manage the handover, collect your records, review what has been filed and flag anything that needs correcting before the FTA finds it.

Do you provide CFO and advisory services?

Yes. Forecasting, cash flow modelling, DCF valuation, scenario planning and bank or investor ready reporting, either inside the Elite package or as a standalone retainer.

My books are behind and I think a filing was missed. Can you help?

That is a common starting point. We quantify the exposure first, bring the records current, and use voluntary disclosure where it applies to limit penalties.

Do you work with free zone companies?

Yes, including designated zone VAT treatment and qualifying free zone person analysis for corporate tax.

Are you registered with the FTA?

Yes. NovaFin is a registered tax agent and can represent your business directly with the authority.
